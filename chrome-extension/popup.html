📋 How to Use

  1. Ensure Hush backend is running on localhost:8501
  2. Join a Google Meet call
  3. Enable captions in Google Meet (CC button)
  4. Click Start Capture to begin analysis
  5. Open Hush website to see AI decisions in real-time

🔒 Privacy Notice

This extension captures visible captions from your Google Meet session and sends them to your local Hush backend for AI analysis.

  • Only visible captions are captured
  • Capture starts only when you click Start
  • No audio or video is accessed
  • Data is sent to your local backend only
  • No external services are contacted
